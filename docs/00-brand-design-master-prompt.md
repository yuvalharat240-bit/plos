# plos — Design Language & Brand System Master Prompt (source)

> Verbatim-condensed source document provided 2026-09-20. Normative for all
> future visual/brand work. Full original wording is preserved in spirit;
> this file groups the 46 numbered sections under their functional headings
> so the rules stay easy to look up.

## Identity

- **Name**: `plos` — always lowercase. Never `PLOS`, `Plos`, `Plos OS`.
  Meaning: Personal Life Operating System. Lowercase communicates *product,
  system, utility* rather than corporate brand.
- **Primary symbol**: `+`. Represents addition, connection, integration,
  bringing fragmented parts of a life together — adding context, not noise.
  Must become a proprietary, recognizable visual asset (geometry, negative
  space, proportion, motion, interaction) — not a default font glyph.
- **Brand idea**: plos is not a chatbot, health app, productivity app,
  finance app, or fitness tracker — it is the connective layer above all of
  them. "One system. Many dimensions of life."

## Design objective & personality

Communicate: intelligence, clarity, precision, trust, calm, sophistication,
technology, humanity, privacy, continuity, simplicity.
Avoid: futuristic/robotic/clinical/childish/gamified/corporate/crypto/
generic-SaaS/stereotypical-AI/luxurious/sterile.

Personality reference: **Apple × Linear × premium personal technology ×
intelligent infrastructure** — principles, never assets, never a copy.
Target impression: *"This feels like the operating system for my life,"*
never *"another AI app."*

## Core principle

**"Complex underneath. Simple above."** Deep, rich, technical infrastructure;
calm, clear, human surface.

## Design tenets

1. **Clarity over decoration** — every element must communicate something.
2. **Calm over stimulation** — no excessive cards/gradients/animation/glow/
   notification overload.
3. **Intelligence over spectacle** — never signal "AI is working" with
   visual effects; let output quality carry it.
4. **Context over metrics** — a number is only as valuable as its context
   (`"6h 14m"` → `"1h06m below baseline"` → `"consider a lighter day"`).
5. **Human control** — never implies plos owns the user's life. Language:
   "Consider," "You may want to," "Based on your recent pattern," "Your
   choice" — never authoritarian, surveillance-toned, or guilt-inducing.

## The `+` system

Used for: brand mark, Add/Connect/Expand/Create actions, visual convergence
of combined context into an insight, and as a motion primitive (transforms
into a connection/node/transition/completed action) — **never overused**;
it must stay special.

## Logo system & geometry

Primary `+`, secondary `plos +`, wordmark `plos`, app icon (must read at
notification/widget/Watch/browser sizes, in monochrome, light and dark).
Requires a **proprietary** plus geometry: stroke width, corner radius, arm
proportions, optical alignment, negative space, min clear space/size, grid,
responsive variants — not a stock `+` character. Direction: refined
geometric plus, subtly softened corners, minimal ornamentation.

## Color

- Neutral foundation (near-white → white → soft gray → graphite → near-black)
  + **one** distinctive accent color, restrained, accessible across light/
  dark/a11y modes, charts, buttons, notifications. No rainbow AI-gradient
  identity (gradients allowed only as minor atmospheric accents).
- Separate **semantic** color system (success/warning/critical/info/neutral/
  disabled), never color-only — always paired with typography/icon/label/
  position/shape for accessibility.

## Typography, spacing, shape, depth

- Modern, highly legible, neutral, quiet; strong hierarchy over decorative
  fonts. Custom wordmark type is fine; product UI uses a highly readable
  system/interface typeface. Must support English + Hebrew + **RTL from day
  one**, not bolted on later.
- Generous whitespace as an active hierarchy signal (large = conceptual
  separation, medium = related sections, small = tightly related info).
- Shape: restrained geometry, moderate radii, minimal borders, subtle depth.
  No glassmorphism-everywhere, no heavy shadows, no floating-card overload.
- Depth via spacing/typography/scale/contrast/subtle elevation — not shadow
  stacking.

## Iconography & data visualization

Icons: simple, geometric, consistent, same family as the `+`; no cartoon or
inconsistent-weight icons. Charts: every visualization must answer a
specific question (trend / baseline / correlation / progress / context) —
never exist just because data is available; no dashboard-style overload.

## AI visual language

No robots, brains, glowing orbs, circuit boards, humanoid avatars, or
cliché AI imagery. AI is expressed through behavior and typography, with
the `+` as the primary AI-interaction motif (`+ Insight`, `+ Action`,
`+ Context`).

## Life Master Agent UI

Not a ChatGPT clone: no giant default chat home screen, no message-bubble
wall, no avatars/typing indicators, no long responses. Hybrid interaction
model: **Ask → Understand → Explain (concise) → Act (optional) → Inspect
(on demand)**. Default view stays concise; depth is opt-in.

## Insight component

Structure: **What** (what changed) → **Why** (evidence) → **What it may
mean** (interpretation with uncertainty) → **What you can do** (optional
next step). Avoid anthropomorphic certainty ("Your body is telling you...").

## Confidence & uncertainty

Use qualitative language ("Likely," "Possible," "Appears related," "Based
on limited data") instead of decorative percentages. Support a "Why am I
seeing this?" inspector (relevant data, time period, reasoning summary,
limitations) — **never** expose raw chain-of-thought.

## Home, personalization, empty states, onboarding

- Home answers *"What matters to me right now?"*, not *"how much data do we
  have?"*: Today state (sleep/energy/mood/recovery) → priorities → calendar
  → one insight → recommended next action → Ask plos.
- Personalization is subtle ("Based on your recent pattern..."), never
  "We know everything about you" — never implies surveillance.
- Empty states explain what's missing, why it matters, and what to do next
  — never a bare "Nothing here!".
- Onboarding is progressive: value prop → primary goals → minimal account
  info → relevant permissions first, then progressively more; never a
  25-permission wall on first launch.

## Connections, privacy UX, professional sharing

- Dedicated "Connected / Available" integration screen: data shared, last
  sync, permission state, disconnect action per provider.
- Privacy controls live in-product (what plos knows / can access / AI can
  use / who can access it), in plain language — legal language stays in
  legal documents.
- Professional-sharing UI must always show who receives access, what's
  shared, expiry, read/write, and a revoke path. Never a default "share
  everything."

## Motion, microinteractions, voice

Motion communicates continuity/cause-effect/hierarchy, not spectacle;
fast, subtle, purposeful; respects Reduce Motion and battery. The `+` can
be a recurring motion primitive.

Microinteractions reinforce understanding, not entertainment — e.g.
connecting an integration animates `+ Connect → Connected`; completing an
action animates `+ → → ✓`. Keep them subtle; the interface must never feel
gamified.

Voice (if built) integrates as "Tell plos," not "Talk to your AI
assistant" — same visual language, not a separate mode.

## Privacy as a visual value

"Your life belongs to you" expressed visually — no surveillance/biometric/
data-center/security-fortress aesthetics; privacy reads as a quiet
architectural property, not a marketing badge.

## Photography & illustration

Real-life, authentic, non-stock imagery if used at all; illustration
secondary, minimal/geometric/abstract, `+`-motif based — no mascots without
strong strategic reason.

## Marketing language (unlocked, not finalized)

Candidate territory only: "plos — Personal Life Operating System" / "Your
life, connected." / "Intelligence for your whole life." Tagline not final.

## Design system architecture

Foundations (color, type, spacing, grid, radius, elevation, motion, a11y) →
Components (buttons, inputs, nav, cards, lists, charts, insights,
notifications, modals, sheets, menus, permission controls, connection
states, agent interactions) → Patterns (onboarding, data connection,
insight presentation, goal creation, journal entry, professional sharing,
subscription, privacy controls, error/empty states) → Templates (Home,
Journal, Goals, Insights, Profile, Connections, Privacy, Agent).

## Cross-cutting requirements

- **Responsive**: iPhone, iPad, web (if built), widgets, notifications,
  Watch (if built) — identity stays coherent across all.
- **Accessibility**: Dynamic Type, VoiceOver, contrast, Reduce Motion,
  larger touch targets, color-independent communication, RTL, keyboard
  nav — part of the design system, not a premium-tier exclusion.
- **Consistency test**: every new feature must answer "Does this follow
  plos's principles (calm, precise, intelligent, human, minimal,
  contextual, controlled, trustworthy)?", not just "does it use the brand
  colors?"

## Explicit anti-patterns ("never look like")

Generic AI SaaS (purple gradient + glowing orb + chatbot); fitness app
(aggressive colors + badges + gamification); medical app (sterile
clinical-blue dashboards); finance app (black/gold stock-overload luxury);
productivity app (dense task-management density); social network
(engagement-driven patterns); cybersecurity product (dark hacker
aesthetic); wellness app (overly soft pastel lifestyle branding). plos must
unify these domains without visually collapsing into any one of them.

## Scalability

The identity must not depend on any one domain (health, fitness, finance,
etc.) — it belongs to the operating-system concept, so it must still work
when the product expands into new life domains.

## Deliverables (for the eventual design phase)

Brand: logo, `+` symbol, wordmark, app icon, clear-space/min-size rules,
mono + light/dark variants. Design system: color/type/spacing/radius/
shadow/icon/motion tokens. Product UI: Home, Journal, Goals, Insights,
Life Master Agent, Connections, Privacy, Profile, Professional sharing,
Onboarding. Docs: brand guidelines, UX principles, component library,
accessibility rules, do/don't examples.

## Design process order (do not skip ahead)

1. Brand concept → 2. `+` geometry → 3. Typography → 4. Color → 5. Spacing/
geometry → 6. Component system → 7. Core flows → 8. Home → 9. Life Master
Agent → 10. Cross-domain coherence test (health + finance + travel +
fitness + productivity together) → 11. Accessibility test → 12. Final
review.

## Final design test (apply before approving any design)

Does it feel intelligent without looking like AI? Premium without looking
luxurious? Technical without looking cold? Personal without looking
intrusive? Simple without feeling simplistic? Powerful without feeling
overwhelming? Does the `+` feel like a real brand asset, not a generic
symbol? Could this identity represent someone's entire life operating
system? If any answer is no, iterate.

## Final creative direction

*"plos should feel like something that could have always existed."* Not a
flashy startup, not another AI interface, not another dashboard — a quiet,
highly refined layer between a person and the complexity of modern life.
Sophisticated technology, calm interface, subtle intelligence, user
firmly in control. The `+` is the shorthand for: **everything in your
life, connected.**
